Snack Planet · Privacy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Snack Planet is a free browser game. We keep this simple: there are no accounts, no ads in this version, and we never ask for your name, email or other personal details.

Anonymous gameplay analytics

To learn where the game is fun and where players get stuck, we use ByteBrew (bytebrew.io), an analytics service for games. When analytics is on, the game sends:

  • A random user ID that ByteBrew creates and keeps in a cookie or browser storage on your device. It is not linked to your name or any account.
  • Gameplay events, for example: the game started, how many minutes you have played, a throw's distance and the zone it landed in, a new creature found, an upgrade bought, a tip shown or completed, and offline coins collected.
  • Device and browser information that comes with any web request, such as browser type, language, screen size, rough country, and the game version. ByteBrew receives your IP address when the game talks to its servers.

We use this only to improve the game. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your saved game

Your progress (coins, upgrades, creatures, settings) is saved in your browser's localStorage on this device. It is not uploaded anywhere. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes it.

How to turn analytics off

Press the button below. The game will not start analytics again in this browser, and if it was running it is stopped the next time the game loads. Your saved game is not affected.

You can also clear this site's cookies and storage in your browser settings, or use your browser's tracking protection. Note that clearing site data deletes your saved game too.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and has no chat or sign-up.
